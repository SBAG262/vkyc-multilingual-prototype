## Officially Valid Documents (OVDs)
The accepted OVDs for KYC are: Passport, Driving Licence, Voter's Identity
Card issued by the Election Commission, PAN card, Job card issued by NREGA
signed by a State Government officer, and the Aadhaar letter or card.
A PAN card is accepted as proof of identity but is NOT accepted as proof
of address. A Driving Licence and Passport serve as both identity and
address proof, provided they are not expired.

## Deemed OVDs for address proof
Where the OVD does not contain the current address, a deemed OVD may be
accepted: a utility bill not more than two months old for electricity,
telephone, piped gas, water or post-paid mobile; a property or municipal
tax receipt; a pension payment order for retired employees; or a letter
of allotment of accommodation from a government employer.
The customer must submit an updated OVD with the current address within
three months of submitting the deemed OVD.

## Liveness and video session requirements
The V-CIP session must be a live, real-time video interaction. The officer
must confirm liveness by asking the customer to perform a random action,
such as turning their head or blinking on request. The officer must vary
the sequence and type of questions asked in each session to establish that
the interaction is live and not a recorded or replayed session.
The officer must capture a clear live photograph of the customer and match
it against the photograph on the submitted OVD.

## Grounds for rejecting a V-CIP session
A session must be rejected if: the video or audio quality is too poor to
confirm identity; the officer observes the customer being prompted,
coached or read a script by another person; the customer's face does not
match the OVD photograph; the customer is not physically present in India
at the time of the session; or the customer refuses to perform the
liveness check. The officer must record the specific reason for any
rejection. Rejection without a recorded reason is not permitted.

## Geo-tagging and location
The customer must be physically located in India during the V-CIP session.
The officer must capture the geo-coordinates of the customer's location
and confirm they fall within India. A session where the customer is
located outside India must be rejected.

## Recording and audit trail
The complete V-CIP session must be recorded with a clear and audible
audio-video feed, and must carry a date and time stamp. The recording is
retained as part of the customer's KYC records in an alteration-proof
form. The officer who conducted the session must be identifiable in the
record.

## Minors
For a customer below the age of ten, the KYC documents of the person who
opens and operates the account are obtained. For a minor above ten who
wishes to operate the account independently, the standard KYC procedure
applies to the minor.

## Assisted and interpreted sessions
Where a language interpretation tool is used, the trained bank officer
remains responsible for conducting the session and for all verification
decisions. The interpretation tool is assistive only and does not replace
the officer's judgment on liveness, identity match or document validity.
